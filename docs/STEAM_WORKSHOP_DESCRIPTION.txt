[h1]Overlord[/h1]
Let Twitch viewers or friends control your RimWorld colonists from a browser.

[b]Requires Harmony.[/b]

[b]No server setup is required.[/b] Overlord uses its official relay by default. Hosts do not need a Twitch account, Twitch token, or server of their own. You can replace the relay address with one you operate yourself.

[h2]First-time setup[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Subscribe to [b]Harmony[/b] and [b]Overlord[/b], enable both, then restart RimWorld.
[*] Load or start a save and open the [b]Overlord[/b] tab.
[*] Choose [b]People in my house[/b] for local friends, or [b]People anywhere / my stream[/b] for an online game.
[/olist]

[b]People in my house[/b]
[list]
[*] Overlord shows the local browser link to share.
[*] Friends type any name and claim a colonist. No Twitch login or relay is used.
[/list]

[b]People anywhere / my stream[/b]
[list]
[*] The official Overlord relay is already filled in.
[*] Press [b]Set me up[/b]. Overlord creates your private room and copies the viewer link automatically.
[*] Only viewers sign in with Twitch. The host never enters a Twitch token.
[*] To use another compatible relay, replace the address before pressing [b]Set me up[/b].
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Assign colonists to Twitch viewers or friends
[*] Browser control panel for needs, health, skills, map, draft, movement, work, and gear
[*] Host controls for sessions, assignments, and permissions
[*] Optional Twitch Toolkit Buy / Story integration
[/list]
